Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

DOZE OPO ("we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of our visitors and customers. This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you visit or purchase from www.dozeopo.com.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order, create an account, or contact our team, we collect necessary business details including:

  • Contact Information: Full name, company/brand name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number.
  • Shipping & Delivery Details: Recipient delivery address, city, state/province, postal code, and country.
  • Payment Information: All payment transactions (credit card, debit card, PayPal) are processed securely through certified PCI-DSS compliant third-party gateways (PayPal, JPMorgan Chase). We do not store full credit card numbers or security CVV codes on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use customer information strictly for legitimate commercial fulfillment purposes:

  • Processing, proofing, manufacturing, and dispatching garment orders and custom tech packs.
  • Generating air express shipping waybills and emailing automated shipment tracking updates.
  • Responding to customer service inquiries, sample requests, and wholesale quotations.
  • Preventing fraudulent transactions and ensuring website security.

3. Data Protection & Sharing Policy

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Your details are shared solely with operational partners necessary to fulfill your order:

  • International courier carriers (FedEx, DHL, UPS) to perform delivery.
  • Payment processors to authorize and settle payments securely.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request that your contact details be deleted from our records. To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@dozeopo.com or jackie@dozeopo.com.
